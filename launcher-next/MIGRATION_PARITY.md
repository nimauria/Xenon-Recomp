## Property families

The legacy Q_PROPERTY getters/setters are carried by the versioned snapshot rather than copied as Qt bindings: runtime/session/capabilities and logs; recovery; profile IDs and limits; theme/accent/corner/background catalogues; notification counts; settings and configured paths; and host-service DTOs. Qt version is deliberately retired. Host architecture/platform/build version, community provider diagnostics and every default/configured-path presentation still require an individual property acceptance pass. These are not silently counted as complete.
